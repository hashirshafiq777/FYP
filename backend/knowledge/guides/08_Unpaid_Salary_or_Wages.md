# Boss/company salary na de to kya steps lein?

1. Apna record banayein: appointment/contract, salary slips, attendance/roster, bank statements, messages, amount and months unpaid, and any resignation/termination notice.
2. Employer/HR ko calm written request bhejein listing the amount and period due, ask for a written payment date, and keep delivery proof. Do not sign a “full and final” receipt unless the amount actually received and terms are understood.
3. If unpaid, contact the relevant provincial labour department/labour inspector or the authority/court named by the applicable wages law. Take your records and ask about filing deadline, eligibility, and required form. Union/worker representative may help where applicable.
4. Do not stop work or resign based only on generic advice; employment status and next steps depend on contract, worker classification, establishment, and local law. Get local labour-law advice promptly because claim periods can be short.

The Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and Industrial Relations Act, 2012 are among possible federal statutes, but application is not universal: provinces have their own labour laws and coverage can depend on the establishment and worker's role. Ask the user for province/city, job type, employer type, and which salary period is unpaid before stating a specific deadline or forum.
