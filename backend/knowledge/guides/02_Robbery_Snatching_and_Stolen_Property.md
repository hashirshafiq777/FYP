# Samaan cheen kar koi bhaag jaye to kya karein?

## Right away
1. Apni jaan aur doosre logon ki safety ko pehle rakhein. Chor/robber ka peecha, pakarne ki koshish, ya muqabla na karein; weapon ya group ho to door safe jagah par chalein.
2. Zakhm ho to medical help lein. Jitni jaldi safe ho, local police/emergency service ko inform karein.
3. Waqt, exact jagah, direction of escape, shakhs/vehicle ka safe description, chori/cheene gaye items, serial/IMEI numbers, aur witnesses note karein. Nearby CCTV ki location note karke footage jaldi preserve karne ki request karein; khud kisi suspect ko confront na karein.
4. Police station mein written report dein, receiving/diary number aur FIR number/copy maangein. Report mein facts sahi aur poore likhwa kar padh lein.
5. Phone/wallet chori ho to bank cards/accounts, SIM, email and device access ko official provider channels se block/secure karein. Passwords change karein from a safe device.
6. Insurance ho to insurer ko policy ki deadline ke andar inform karein and claim requirements poochein.

Pakistan Penal Code (PPC) mein theft (including sections 378–379) aur robbery (sections 390–392) alag defined offences hain; facts decide karte hain ke kaunsi provision apply hoti hai. Victim khud section guess na kare—police/advocate ko accurate facts de. FIR ke baad copy aur investigation officer details sambhal kar rakhein.
