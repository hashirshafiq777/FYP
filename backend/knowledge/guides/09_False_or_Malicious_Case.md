# Ghalat FIR/case ho jaye to kya karein?

1. FIR/complaint ki copy, exact sections, police station, date, and investigation officer details get and preserve karein. FIR registration itself guilt prove nahi karti.
2. Us district ke criminal advocate ko jaldi dikhayein. Arrest ka risk ho to same day batayein so counsel can assess whether pre-arrest bail is available; if arrested, counsel can address post-arrest bail, production/remand, and court dates. Bail depends on the statute, allegation, record and court.
3. Apni defence ke original proof secure karein: messages, location data, travel/work receipts, CCTV, medical records, contracts, and real witnesses. Do not edit/delete data, coach witnesses, contact/threaten complainant, flee, or publish a counter-story online.
4. Every police/court appearance and filing ka record rakhein. Any written statement should be read, understood, and discussed with counsel before signing; never sign a blank paper.
5. After reviewing the investigation and evidence, advocate can advise whether to seek bail, challenge proceedings, request further investigation, or pursue another remedy. Counter-case or defamation action is not automatic; do not file allegations you cannot support.

Relevant law includes CrPC sections 154, 497–498 (FIR/bail process and court powers), the particular offence sections in the FIR, and constitutional safeguards under Article 10. Verify current consolidated law and provincial amendments with local counsel.
