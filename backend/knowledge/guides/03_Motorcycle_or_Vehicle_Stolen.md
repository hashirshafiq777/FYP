# Bike ya gaari chori ho jaye to pehle kya karein?

1. Agar theft abhi ho rahi ho, khud chor ka peecha ya muqabla na karein. Safe jagah se police ko foran report karein.
2. Nearest police station ko written complaint dein. Registration/number plate, make/model, colour, chassis and engine numbers, last location/time, keys, tracking-device details, and ownership/registration proof provide karein. FIR/complaint number and copy/receipt zaroor lein.
3. Nearby CCTV locations, witnesses, tracker location, and any suspicious vehicle details police ko dein. Tracker signal par akelay ja kar recovery ki koshish na karein; police ko location pass karein.
4. Insurer ko turant inform karein and policy ke mutabiq required report/certificates aur deadlines confirm karein. Registration/vehicle authority ko bhi required record update ke bare mein poochein.
5. Saare documents, police contacts, investigator name, dates and follow-up receipts ka folder rakhein. Kisi unknown person ko “recovery fee” na bhejein.

PPC theft provisions may apply; exact offence depends on facts. FIR aur investigation process CrPC ke tehat hota hai. Registration, insurance, and reporting process local authority aur policy ke hisaab se vary kar sakta hai; city/province batayein.
