# Hamla ho to kya karein? Self-defence ki had kya hai?

## During an immediate attack
1. Pehle jaan bachayein: mauqa ho to door ho kar safe public place mein chalein, help bulayein, aur local emergency/police service ko contact karein. Kisi ko punish ya chase karne ki koshish na karein.
2. Sirf immediate danger se bachne ke liye zaroori protective action karein. Khatra rukne par force bhi rok dein. Weapon use karne ya badla lene ke tareeqe mat apnayein.
3. Injury ho to foran medical examination/treatment lein. Doctor ko incident ka sahi waqt aur history batayein; medical papers sambhal lein.
4. Safe hone ke baad police ko written facts dein; time, place, witnesses, photos, messages, CCTV and medical documents preserve karein. Injuries ki original photos and copies alag rakhein.

## Legal rule
PPC sections 96–106 address the right of private defence of person/property. Section 99 places important limits, including that more harm than necessary must not be inflicted and the right does not apply in every situation. Sections 102 and 105 address when the right begins and ends. Whether a particular response was lawful depends on immediate danger, available public protection, proportionality, and evidence. “Self-defence” is not blanket permission to retaliate, carry/use a weapon, or continue force after danger ends. Get a criminal advocate promptly if anyone was injured or a case/FIR may be filed.
