# Police kisi ko le jaye ya arrest kare to kya karein?

## If you are the person being stopped/arrested
1. Physical resistance, threats, or attempts to escape can make the situation more dangerous. Stay as calm as possible and ask which police station, the reason/grounds, and the officer's name/badge details.
2. Clearly ask to contact a lawyer and a trusted family member. Do not sign a blank paper or a statement you have not read/understood; tell your lawyer about any pressure, injury, or medication needs.
3. Keep track of the time and place of arrest and where you are taken. Request that family/lawyer be told the police station and case/FIR details.
4. Ask the lawyer to check the FIR, legal basis for arrest, production/remand date, medical needs, and any bail remedy. Do not assume every arrest without a warrant is automatically unlawful; the applicable power and facts need legal review.

## If a friend/family member is taken
Call a local criminal advocate immediately. Write down the time, place, witnesses, vehicle/official identification, and any words said; contact nearby police stations and request confirmation of custody and case number. Keep communications factual. If whereabouts are concealed or access is refused, the advocate can assess urgent court remedies. Do not confront officers or obstruct a lawful process.

Constitution Article 10 provides safeguards: an arrested person must be informed of the grounds as soon as may be and may consult/be defended by a lawyer of choice; a person in custody generally must be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours, excluding necessary journey time. CrPC sections 61 and 167 address detention and remand during investigation. Apply these rules to the actual case with a lawyer; special statutes can change procedure.
