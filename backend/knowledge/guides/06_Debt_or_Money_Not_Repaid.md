# Koi qarz/paisay wapas na de to kya karein?

1. Proof ikattha karein: written agreement/receipt, bank or wallet transfer, messages, due date, part-payments, and any written admission. Keep original records and a clear timeline.
2. Calm written demand bhejein: amount, kis basis par due hai, payment date, and a reasonable reply deadline. Delivery proof save karein. If both sides agree, put any instalment plan and signatures/acknowledgment in writing.
3. Local civil advocate se documents review karwa kar legal notice, mediation, or civil recovery suit ka route poochein. Limitation periods and court/jurisdiction depend on the claim and dates; delay na karein.
4. Do not threaten, publicly shame, seize property, or use force to recover money. A mere failure to repay a genuine loan is not automatically a criminal offence; a lawyer can assess whether evidence supports a separate offence such as deception from the outset.
5. If threats or violence occur, handle safety and report that separately; do not turn a civil payment dispute into a fabricated criminal complaint.

Relevant law may include the Contract Act, 1872, Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, Limitation Act, 1908, and Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984, plus the agreement's terms. The right remedy requires reviewing the exact transaction and dates.

Official statutes are available through Pakistan Code: https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/
