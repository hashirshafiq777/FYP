# The State v. Imran Ali (Zainab Ansari Murder Case, Kasur)

**Court:** Anti-Terrorism Court Lahore (trial); Lahore High Court and Supreme Court of Pakistan (appeals) **Year:** 2018 **Citation:** citation not confirmed

## Background / Facts
Zainab Amin Ansari, a girl of about six or seven years, went missing in Kasur, Punjab, on 4 January 2018 while her parents were abroad for Umrah; she had gone to a nearby house for Quran lessons. Her body was found on 9 January 2018 in a garbage heap in the city. The post-mortem showed she had been raped and strangled. CCTV footage showing a man leading her away was circulated widely, and violent protests broke out in Kasur in which two people were killed by police firing. Kasur had already been the scene of a large child sexual abuse scandal in 2015, and several other young girls had been killed in similar circumstances in the preceding year. On 23 January 2018, police arrested Imran Ali, a 24-year-old neighbour, after DNA matching linked him to Zainab's case and to several earlier cases of rape and murder of minor girls in the area. The Supreme Court had taken suo motu notice and the Lahore High Court Chief Justice monitored the investigation.

## Legal Issues
- Whether DNA evidence, CCTV footage and other circumstantial evidence proved beyond reasonable doubt that Imran Ali kidnapped, raped and murdered Zainab.
- Whether the offence fell within the definition of terrorism under the Anti-Terrorism Act 1997 so as to be tried by an Anti-Terrorism Court.
- Whether the sentence of death on multiple counts was appropriate.

## Decision
The trial was conducted inside Kot Lakhpat Jail, Lahore, by an Anti-Terrorism Court and was completed in four days. On 17 February 2018 the court convicted Imran Ali and sentenced him to death on four counts (kidnapping, rape, murder and terrorism), together with life imprisonment under Section 377 and a further term of imprisonment and fine for concealing evidence. The Lahore High Court dismissed his appeal in March 2018, and the Supreme Court dismissed his further appeal in June 2018. His mercy petition was rejected by the President. He was also tried and convicted in several of the other child murder cases. Imran Ali was hanged in Kot Lakhpat Jail, Lahore, on 17 October 2018. A request by Zainab's father that the execution be carried out publicly was not granted.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- Section 302(b), Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (qatl-e-amd, intentional murder)
- Section 364-A, Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (kidnapping or abducting a person under the age of fourteen)
- Section 376, Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (rape)
- Section 377, Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (unnatural offences)
- Section 201, Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (causing disappearance of evidence)
- Section 7, Anti-Terrorism Act 1997 (punishment for acts of terrorism)
- Article 164, Qanun-e-Shahadat Order 1984 (use of modern devices and techniques, relevant to DNA and CCTV evidence)

## Why It Matters
The case was a turning point for child protection in Pakistan. It led directly to the Zainab Alert, Response and Recovery Act 2020, which created a national alert system and a dedicated agency for missing children, and it prompted amendments increasing penalties for child sexual abuse. It also demonstrated the use of DNA databases and forensic science (through the Punjab Forensic Science Agency) to identify serial offenders. The speed of the trial raised questions about fair trial rights, but the conviction was upheld at every stage.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
January 2018 mein Kasur mein chhe saal ki Zainab ko aghwa kar ke ziyadati ke baad qatal kar diya gaya aur us ki laash kachre ke dher se mili. Poore mulk mein ghussa phela. Police ne DNA ke zariye Imran Ali naami parosi ko giraftar kiya jo pehle bhi kai bachiyon ke qatal mein mulawwis tha. Anti-Terrorism Court ne chaar din mein muqadma sun kar usay chaar baar sazaye maut sunai, jo High Court aur Supreme Court ne barqarar rakhi, aur October 2018 mein usay phansi de di gayi. Is case ke baad "Zainab Alert" qanoon banaya gaya.
