# Mst. Kaneez Fatima v. Wali Muhammad (Talaq Notice Case)

**Court:** Supreme Court of Pakistan **Year:** 1993 **Citation:** PLD 1993 SC 901

## Background / Facts
Section 7 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 requires a husband who pronounces talaq to give written notice to the chairman of the Union Council and a copy to the wife, after which the chairman constitutes an Arbitration Council for reconciliation; the talaq does not become effective until ninety days after the notice is received by the chairman. Failure to give notice is punishable. In the years after the Zina Ordinance of 1979, a serious problem arose: husbands pronounced talaq orally without giving the statutory notice, the wife remarried believing herself divorced, and then the first husband or others accused her of zina or bigamy on the basis that the earlier marriage still existed because the notice had not been given. The facts of Kaneez Fatima's case arose out of such a dispute between her and Wali Muhammad about the status of a talaq and the validity of a subsequent marriage, and the question reached the Supreme Court because High Courts had taken conflicting views on whether a talaq without notice was effective.

## Legal Issues
- Is a talaq pronounced by the husband effective in law if no notice is given to the chairman of the Union Council under Section 7 of the MFLO?
- Does Section 7 merely impose a penalty for non-compliance, or does it also govern the time when the divorce takes effect?
- What is the status of a woman who remarries after a talaq for which no notice was given?

## Decision
The Supreme Court held that Section 7 of the MFLO is mandatory and that a talaq which is not notified to the chairman does not become effective; the marriage is deemed to continue until the procedure under Section 7 is followed and the ninety-day period expires. The Court reasoned that the Ordinance was enacted to regulate divorce, protect wives from arbitrary and secret repudiation and ensure attempts at reconciliation, and that the statute could not be read as leaving the effect of talaq to the husband's unverified oral assertion. The Court observed that the consequence of this interpretation must not be used to prosecute women for zina or bigamy where they honestly believed themselves divorced, and later cases and the 2006 amendments addressed that risk. The decision confirmed the view earlier taken in Syed Ali Nawaz Gardezi v. Lt. Col. Muhammad Yusuf (PLD 1963 SC 51) that compliance with Section 7 is necessary for a talaq to take effect.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 7(1) (written notice of talaq to the chairman and the wife)
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 7(2) (penalty for failure to give notice)
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 7(3) (talaq effective ninety days after receipt of notice unless revoked)
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 7(4) (Arbitration Council for reconciliation)
- Offence of Zina (Enforcement of Hudood) Ordinance 1979 (discussed in relation to prosecutions of remarried women)

## Why It Matters
Kaneez Fatima settled the long-standing dispute over whether the Section 7 notice is a formality or a condition of an effective divorce, holding that it is a condition. The rule protects wives from being divorced secretly and ensures that divorce is documented through the Union Council, which also matters for proof of divorce, maintenance during iddat and remarriage. It is cited in nearly every case about the validity and timing of talaq in Pakistan, and the effectiveness certificate issued by the Union Council is a direct result of this interpretation.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
Qanoon ke mutabiq shohar ko talaq dene ke baad Union Council ke chairman ko likhit notice dena zaroori hai, aur talaq nabbay din baad muassar hoti hai. Is case mein sawal tha ke agar shohar sirf zubani talaq de aur notice na de to kya talaq ho gayi? Supreme Court ne 1993 mein kaha ke notice ke baghair talaq qanooni tor par muassar nahi hoti aur nikah qaim rehta hai. Is ka maqsad aurat ko chhupi hui ya zubani talaq se bachana aur sulah ka mauqa dena hai. Isi liye aaj Union Council se talaq ka certificate lena zaroori samjha jata hai.
