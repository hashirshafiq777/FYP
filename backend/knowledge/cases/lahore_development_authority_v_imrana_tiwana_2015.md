# Lahore Development Authority v. Ms. Imrana Tiwana (Signal-Free Corridor / Environmental Impact Case)

**Court:** Supreme Court of Pakistan (on appeal from the Lahore High Court) **Year:** 2015 **Citation:** 2015 SCMR 1739 (Supreme Court); the Lahore High Court full bench judgment is reported as PLD 2015 Lahore 522

## Background / Facts
In 2014 the Lahore Development Authority (LDA) and the Punjab government began a project to convert a stretch of roads in central Lahore, including Jail Road, Main Boulevard Gulberg and the area around the Lahore canal, into a "signal-free corridor" with underpasses, flyovers and road widening. The project was started before an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) under the Punjab Environmental Protection Act 1997 had been completed and approved, and it involved cutting large numbers of mature trees and altering a green belt protected by the Lahore Canal Heritage Park Act 2013. Imrana Tiwana, an architect and civil society activist, and other citizens filed a public interest petition in the Lahore High Court under Article 199, arguing that the project violated environmental law, the heritage park law and the fundamental rights to life and a healthy environment recognised in Shehla Zia v. WAPDA. A full bench of the Lahore High Court, in a judgment authored by Justice Syed Mansoor Ali Shah, declared the project illegal for want of a valid EIA and for breach of the governing statutes, and set aside the approvals. LDA and the Punjab government appealed to the Supreme Court.

## Legal Issues
- Could a major public infrastructure project lawfully begin before approval of an Environmental Impact Assessment under the Punjab Environmental Protection Act 1997?
- To what extent can courts review executive development decisions on environmental grounds, and what is the proper remedy where procedures have been violated but the project is already well advanced?
- How should the principles of sustainable development, the precautionary principle and the public trust doctrine be applied in Pakistan?

## Decision
The Supreme Court set aside the Lahore High Court's judgment to the extent that it had stopped the project, and allowed the project to proceed, but it did so subject to conditions, including compliance with environmental requirements, the completion and approval of the EIA process, tree plantation and mitigation measures, and oversight to protect the canal heritage area. The Court accepted that the fundamental rights to life and dignity include environmental protection and that the authorities had been careless in beginning the work before the EIA, but it held that the courts should be cautious about substituting their own view for that of the executive on matters of urban planning and policy, and that stopping a public project at an advanced stage would cause disproportionate loss to the public. The Court's judgment, authored by Justice Mian Saqib Nisar, discussed the limits of judicial review in policy matters while affirming the environmental principles laid down in Shehla Zia and the High Court's reasoning on sustainable development.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- Article 9, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (right to life, including a healthy environment)
- Article 14, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (dignity)
- Article 199, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (High Court writ jurisdiction)
- Article 185, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (appellate jurisdiction of the Supreme Court)
- Punjab Environmental Protection Act 1997, Section 12 (initial environmental examination and environmental impact assessment required before commencing a project)
- Lahore Canal Heritage Park Act 2013
- Lahore Development Authority Act 1975
- Rio Declaration 1992, Principle 15 (precautionary principle), referred to as persuasive

## Why It Matters
The Imrana Tiwana litigation is the leading modern Pakistani case on judicial review of urban development projects on environmental grounds. The Lahore High Court's full bench judgment is widely cited for the public trust doctrine, sustainable development and the mandatory nature of the EIA requirement, while the Supreme Court's decision shows the balance the courts try to strike between enforcing environmental law and avoiding interference with executive policy. Together they are essential reading for environmental law and administrative law in Pakistan.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
Lahore mein hukumat ne Jail Road aur Gulberg ke ilaqay mein signal-free corridor banana shuru kiya, lekin qanoon ke mutabiq pehle maholiyati jaiza (EIA) manzoor karwaye baghair, aur is ke liye bohat se darakht kaate gaye. Imrana Tiwana aur doosron ne Lahore High Court mein case kiya aur High Court ne project ko ghair qanooni qarar de kar rok diya. Supreme Court ne 2015 mein project ko shara'it ke saath jari rakhne ki ijazat di, lekin saath hi kaha ke maholiyati qanoon par amal zaroori hai aur zindagi ke haq mein saaf mahol ka haq shamil hai. Yeh case maholiyati qanoon aur adalat ke ikhtiyar ki hudood dono ke liye ahem hai.
