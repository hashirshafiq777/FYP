# Mst. Humaira Mehmood v. The State (Free-Will Marriage Case)

**Court:** Lahore High Court **Year:** 1999 **Citation:** PLD 1999 Lahore 494

## Background / Facts
Humaira Mehmood, an adult woman and daughter of a member of the Punjab Provincial Assembly, married Mehmood Butt of her own choice in 1997. Her family disapproved and arranged a forced marriage (nikah) of Humaira with her cousin Moazzam Ghayas; this marriage was recorded with a date earlier than her marriage to Mehmood Butt in order to make it appear that her own marriage was a second, invalid marriage. Humaira and her husband fled, and the family lodged criminal cases accusing Mehmood Butt of abduction and both of them of zina under the Hudood Ordinance. The couple was arrested in Karachi, allegedly with the help of police acting on the family's influence, and Humaira was kept in confinement. She petitioned the Lahore High Court for the quashing of the criminal cases and for protection. The case ran parallel to the earlier Saima Waheed case (Hafiz Abdul Waheed v. Asma Jehangir, PLD 1997 Lahore 301), in which the Lahore High Court had upheld by majority the marriage of an adult Muslim woman without her guardian's consent.

## Legal Issues
- Can an adult (sui juris) Muslim woman contract a valid marriage of her own free will without the consent of her wali (guardian)?
- Was the alleged earlier marriage with her cousin, recorded with a back-dated nikahnama and without her free consent, valid?
- Should the abduction and zina cases registered at the instance of the family be quashed as an abuse of the process of law?

## Decision
Justice Tassaduq Hussain Jillani of the Lahore High Court allowed the petition. The Court held that under Islamic law and the law of Pakistan an adult Muslim woman has the right to marry a person of her choice and that the consent of the wali is not required for the validity of the marriage. It found that the marriage with the cousin was a sham, entered into without Humaira's consent and manipulated by back-dating, and that it could not override her valid marriage to Mehmood Butt. The Court quashed the FIRs for abduction and zina, holding that they had been registered mala fide to harass the couple, and criticised the role of the police in the arrests and confinement. The Court also referred to the fundamental rights to life, liberty and dignity and observed that forced marriage violates the Constitution and Islamic injunctions.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- Section 561-A, Code of Criminal Procedure 1898 (inherent powers of the High Court to quash proceedings)
- Offence of Zina (Enforcement of Hudood) Ordinance 1979, Sections 10 and 16 (zina and enticing a woman, as charged in the FIRs)
- Section 365-B, Pakistan Penal Code 1860 (kidnapping or abducting a woman to compel her for marriage)
- Articles 9, 14 and 35, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (right to life and liberty, dignity, protection of marriage and family)
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 5 (registration of marriages)
- Article 199, Constitution of Pakistan 1973

## Why It Matters
Together with the Saima Waheed case, later affirmed by the Supreme Court in Hafiz Abdul Waheed v. Mrs. Asma Jehangir (PLD 2004 SC 219), this judgment settled that an adult Muslim woman's marriage of choice is valid without the guardian's consent. It also recognised the use of criminal law (abduction and zina complaints) by families as a tool of coercion and directed that such complaints be treated with suspicion. The reasoning has been followed in numerous habeas corpus and quashing petitions by couples who marry against family wishes.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
Humaira Mehmood ne apni marzi se Mehmood Butt se shadi ki, lekin us ke khandan ne zabardasti us ka nikah cousin se karwa diya aur purani tareekh dal kar kaha ke pehla nikah wahi tha. Phir khandan ne Mehmood Butt par aghwa aur dono par zina ke muqadmay bana diye. Lahore High Court ne 1999 mein kaha ke balig Musalman aurat apni marzi se shadi kar sakti hai aur wali ki ijazat zaroori nahi, zabardasti ka nikah jaiz nahi, aur khandan ke banaye huay muqadmay khatam kar diye. Yeh faisla pasand ki shadi ke haq ka bunyadi faisla hai.
