# Mst. Beena v. Raja Muhammad (Child Custody and Welfare of Minor)

**Court:** Supreme Court of Pakistan **Year:** 2019 (judgment reported in 2020) **Citation:** 2020 SCMR 1 (not confirmed)

## Background / Facts
Beena was the mother of a minor child from her marriage to Raja Muhammad. After the marriage ended, the child remained with the mother, and the father applied for custody. The mother had remarried. The Guardian Court and the appellate courts below awarded custody of the child to the father, relying largely on the rule found in classical Hanafi texts and older case law that a mother who remarries a person not related to the child within the prohibited degrees loses her preferential right of custody (hizanat). The mother appealed to the Supreme Court, arguing that the courts had applied the remarriage rule mechanically without examining what was actually in the welfare of the child, who had lived with her since birth. The case was heard by a bench that included Justice Qazi Faez Isa, who authored the judgment (authorship reported, not confirmed).

## Legal Issues
- Is the remarriage of the mother an automatic disqualification from custody of a minor child, or only one factor to be considered?
- What is the governing test for custody disputes under the Guardians and Wards Act 1890?
- How should courts balance the rules of Muslim personal law on hizanat with the statutory requirement that the welfare of the minor is paramount?

## Decision
The Supreme Court allowed the mother's appeal and restored custody to her. The Court held that under Section 17 of the Guardians and Wards Act 1890 the welfare of the minor is the paramount consideration, and that the rules of personal law on hizanat, including the mother's remarriage, are relevant factors but do not operate as rigid disqualifications. It held that the courts below had erred by treating remarriage as decisive without assessing the child's age, attachment, emotional needs and the actual circumstances of both households. The Court emphasised that a young child's separation from the mother who has raised the child can itself be against the child's welfare, and that the father's right of visitation should be protected. The judgment also referred to Pakistan's obligations under the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- Guardians and Wards Act 1890, Section 17 (matters to be considered by the court in appointing a guardian; welfare of the minor)
- Guardians and Wards Act 1890, Section 25 (return of a ward to the custody of the guardian)
- Guardians and Wards Act 1890, Section 7 (power of the court to make an order as to guardianship)
- West Pakistan Family Courts Act 1964, Section 5 and Schedule (jurisdiction of family courts over custody and guardianship)
- Article 185(3), Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (leave to appeal to the Supreme Court)
- United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child 1989, Article 3 (best interests of the child), referred to as persuasive

## Why It Matters
The case is a frequently cited modern authority confirming that in custody disputes the welfare of the child overrides the technical rules of hizanat, including the mother's remarriage. It has been relied upon by family courts and High Courts to reject rigid applications of personal law in favour of a case-by-case assessment of the child's best interests. It also reflects the trend in Pakistani family law toward reading the Guardians and Wards Act in light of international child rights standards.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
Is case mein ek maa ne doosri shadi kar li thi, aur neechli adalaton ne sirf is wajah se bachay ki custody walid ko de di ke purane qanoon ke mutabiq doosri shadi karne wali maa custody ka haq kho deti hai. Supreme Court ne kaha ke Guardians and Wards Act ke tahat sab se ahem cheez bachay ki bhalai (welfare) hai, aur maa ki doosri shadi sirf ek pehlu hai, khud-ba-khud na-ahliyat nahi. Court ne custody maa ko wapas di aur kaha ke walid ko bachay se milne ka haq hoga. Yeh faisla custody ke muqadmaat mein bachay ki bhalai ko sab se upar rakhne ki misaal hai.
