# Dr. Mobashir Hassan v. Federation of Pakistan (NRO Case)

**Court:** Supreme Court of Pakistan **Year:** 2009 (short order of 16 December 2009; detailed judgment reported in 2010) **Citation:** PLD 2010 SC 265

## Background / Facts
On 5 October 2007, President Pervez Musharraf promulgated the National Reconciliation Ordinance 2007 (NRO) as part of an understanding with Benazir Bhutto's Pakistan Peoples Party ahead of her return to Pakistan. The Ordinance allowed the withdrawal of criminal and corruption cases registered between 1 January 1986 and 12 October 1999 against politicians, bureaucrats and others if the cases were found to be politically motivated. Thousands of cases, including corruption references against Asif Ali Zardari and the Swiss money-laundering proceedings, were withdrawn under it. Dr. Mobashir Hassan, a founding member of the PPP, and others challenged the NRO under Article 184(3). Parliament did not pass the NRO into law, and the government chose not to defend it before the Court. A seventeen-member full court headed by Chief Justice Iftikhar Muhammad Chaudhry heard the petitions.

## Legal Issues
- Was the NRO consistent with the fundamental right of equality before law (Article 25) and with the constitutional scheme of accountability?
- Could an Ordinance withdraw pending criminal and corruption cases for a specific class of persons during a specific period?
- What should happen to the cases withdrawn under the NRO, including the proceedings in Switzerland?

## Decision
On 16 December 2009 the Court unanimously declared the NRO void ab initio, holding that it was discriminatory because it benefited a particular class of persons for a particular period, violated Articles 4, 8, 25, 62, 63, 89, 175 and 227 of the Constitution, and offended the principle that the law should be the same for all. All cases, inquiries, references and proceedings withdrawn or closed under the NRO were revived and placed back in the position they were in on 5 October 2007. The Court directed the Federal Government to write to the Swiss authorities to revive proceedings concerning accounts allegedly linked to Asif Ali Zardari, which had been withdrawn by the then Attorney General Malik Qayyum, and ordered that NAB monitor all revived cases. The refusal of Prime Minister Yousaf Raza Gilani to write the letter to the Swiss authorities led to his conviction for contempt of court and his disqualification in 2012.

## Laws and Provisions Involved
- National Reconciliation Ordinance 2007 (Ordinance No. LX of 2007), in particular Sections 2, 6 and 7
- Article 4, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (right to be dealt with in accordance with law)
- Article 8, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (laws inconsistent with fundamental rights void)
- Article 25, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (equality of citizens)
- Articles 62 and 63, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (qualifications and disqualifications of parliamentarians)
- Article 89, Constitution of Pakistan 1973 (power of President to promulgate Ordinances)
- Article 184(3), Constitution of Pakistan 1973
- Section 494, Code of Criminal Procedure 1898 (withdrawal from prosecution)
- National Accountability Ordinance 1999

## Why It Matters
The NRO judgment established that Parliament or the President cannot grant a blanket amnesty to a selected group of persons from criminal and corruption charges, because such a law violates equality before law. It became one of the most politically significant judgments of the post-2009 Supreme Court, leading to the removal of a Prime Minister for contempt and years of conflict between the judiciary and the PPP government. It is frequently cited on Article 25 and on the limits of ordinance-making power.

## Simple Explanation (Roman Urdu)
2007 mein Musharraf ne NRO naami ordinance jari kiya jis ke tahat siyasatdanon aur afsaron ke khilaf 1986 se 1999 tak ke corruption aur criminal cases khatam kar diye gaye. Supreme Court ne December 2009 mein satrah judges ke faisle se kaha ke yeh qanoon shuru se hi kaaladam (void) hai kyun ke yeh sirf khas logon ko faida deta hai aur aain ke Article 25 (sab ke liye barabar qanoon) ke khilaf hai. Tamam khatam kiye gaye cases dobara khul gaye, aur Swiss cases dobara kholne ke liye khat likhne se inkar par Wazir-e-Azam Gilani ko tauheen-e-adalat mein saza hui.
